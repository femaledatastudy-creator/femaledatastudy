Prototype for ethics review. This page shows the layout and interactions of the platform participants will use within the Qualtrics survey. The results in the first layer follow the platform's rules. The second layer ("Add what only AI can guess") shows fixed example results in this prototype; in the study, these are generated by an AI service from the options selected. No data entered on this page is stored or sent anywhere.

What they can know about you

Choose what you log and how long. The fan opens to the right in three certainty bands: nearest to you, red, is what they are most sure of; then blue; then green, a vague guess. The solid inner half of each band is what anyone can safely work out. Add AI and a lighter half grows just outside it, the extra it reaches for, often from data you never typed in.
